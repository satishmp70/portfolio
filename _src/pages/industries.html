How we work with regulated data

When your software handles health, financial or personal data, we plan for it from the first sprint rather than bolting it on before launch:

  • Data minimisation: we collect and store only what the product needs, with clear retention rules.
  • Access control: role-based permissions, audit logs and separation between customers’ data.
  • Encryption: TLS in transit and encryption at rest for sensitive fields and backups.
  • Hosting choices: data residency options (for example, keeping Indian customer data in an Indian cloud region) agreed with you up front.

We are engineers, not lawyers: for formal compliance sign-off we work alongside your legal or compliance advisers. Learn more about how we deliver or talk to us about your requirements.
