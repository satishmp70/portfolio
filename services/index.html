OUR SERVICES

What we build

Custom SaaS Development

Multi-tenant platforms with billing, analytics, and role-based access built in.

We build subscription software products end to end: tenant isolation, user roles, subscription billing, admin dashboards, usage analytics and the APIs your customers integrate with — designed so the first version can grow without a rewrite.

Best for: Founders launching a SaaS MVP, and companies turning an internal tool into a product
Typical timeline: 4–6 weeks for a focused MVP; 8–12+ weeks for larger platforms
What's included
  • Multi-tenant architecture & RBAC
  • Stripe / Paddle billing
  • Real-time analytics
  • Cloud microservices

Headless CMS Architecture

Decoupled content platforms for fast, omnichannel publishing.

We move marketing sites and content-heavy platforms to a headless CMS such as Strapi, Contentful or Sanity, with a fast Next.js front end, editor-friendly workflows and live preview — so your team publishes without developers and pages load quickly.

Best for: Marketing teams outgrowing WordPress, and multi-language or multi-site brands
Typical timeline: 3–5 weeks for a standard site; longer with large content migrations
What's included
  • Contentful, Strapi & Sanity setup
  • Live preview & editorial workflows
  • Multilingual content
  • GraphQL / REST content APIs

Legacy Modernization

Move monolithic systems to modern cloud architectures with zero data loss.

We modernize ageing applications in stages: assess the current system, stabilise it, then migrate modules and data to a modern, containerised architecture behind the scenes — keeping the business running throughout instead of a risky big-bang rewrite.

Best for: Businesses running on old PHP, .NET or desktop systems that have become hard to change
Typical timeline: 6–8 weeks for a first modernised module; larger programmes run in phases
What's included
  • Monolith to microservices
  • Data migration & schema cleanup
  • Zero-downtime cutover
  • CI/CD & containerization

Best before a big decision. We audit your code, infrastructure and security, and deliver a prioritised roadmap.

  • Code & security audit
  • Scaling and cost review
  • Written, prioritised recommendations

What affects the cost of a software project?

  • Scope: the number of user roles, screens and features in the first release.
  • Integrations: payment gateways, ERPs, CRMs, messaging (SMS, email, WhatsApp) and other third-party APIs.
  • Data migration: moving records or content from an existing system without losing anything.
  • Security & compliance: requirements such as India’s DPDP Act, HIPAA for US healthcare data, or audit logging.
  • Timeline: a fast-track launch needs a larger team working in parallel.

Third-party costs such as cloud hosting, domains and paid APIs are billed at actual cost, and fees are exclusive of GST. Full details are in our terms & conditions.

FAQ

Questions about working with us

Which engagement model is right for my project?

Choose a fixed-scope project when requirements are clear and you want a fixed set of deliverables; a dedicated engineering pod when your roadmap is evolving and you want senior engineers inside your sprints; or an architecture review when you need an expert assessment of existing code, security or scalability before committing to a larger build.

Will you sign an NDA before we share our idea?

Yes. We are happy to sign your NDA (or provide ours) before a detailed discussion. Our terms also commit both parties to confidentiality for three years after an engagement, and indefinitely for trade secrets and personal data.

Can you take over or improve an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a short code and infrastructure review, document the risks we find, and then either stabilise and extend the existing system or plan a staged modernization — without a risky big-bang rewrite.

What happens after launch?

Every release includes a 30-day warranty during which we fix reproducible defects at no extra cost. After that, you can move to a support plan covering monitoring, security patching, backups and ongoing improvements — see Cloud & 24/7 Support.

Which technologies do you work with?

Mainly TypeScript, React and Next.js on the front end; Node.js, Python (FastAPI) and Go on the back end; PostgreSQL, Redis and MongoDB for data; and AWS, Vercel, Cloudflare and Docker for hosting. See the full technology stack and how we choose it.
